## One-liner
Your Zoom & Fathom recordings, automatically published to YouTube and your course platform — hands-free.

## The problem it solves
Educators and coaches who teach live face the same grind after every session: download the
recording, wait; upload to YouTube, set title and privacy; add it to the right course and module
in the LMS; then clear it off Zoom before storage fills. It's 15–30 minutes of babysitting per
recording, easy to forget or misfile, and it doesn't scale with daily classes.

## Who it's for (ICP)
- Online educators, coaching institutes, and course creators recording on **Zoom / Fathom** and publishing to **YouTube + an LMS**
- Webinar-driven businesses and cohort programs with a steady stream of recordings
- Teaching organizations (spiritual, academic, skills) maintaining structured course libraries
- Sweet spot: anyone producing **several recordings a week** who moves them by hand today

## Value / time saved
- **~20 minutes saved per recording**, fully hands-off — daily classes get **hours back each week**
- **Zero missed or misfiled uploads** — every session lands on the right channel and course
- **Storage stays clean** — recordings removed from Zoom once safely published
- **Consistent titles, privacy, playlists** — no manual formatting each time

## The jaw-drop: speed
- A full **1 GB video moves in ~90 seconds** — **≈ 60s to download** from the source, **≈ 30s to upload** to YouTube.
- A whole day's recordings are done in minutes.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Connect** — link Zoom & Fathom recordings, YouTube channel(s), and your LMS (via its API). One-time, secure.
2. **Set rules** — map which recordings go to which channel / course / module, with title and privacy defaults.
3. **Relax** — on a schedule or one click, each new recording is routed end-to-end and logged in the dashboard.

## Feature highlights
- **Sources:** Zoom **and** Fathom, pulled automatically.
- **Destinations:** YouTube channels **and** LMS (LMS push via API).
- **Schedule & logs:** run daily for regular sessions; every step is visible in the dashboard logs.
- **Staff & permissions:** add team members with view/edit access — simple enough that *anyone can run it, even your kid*.
- **Delete from Zoom, in-dashboard:** remove published recordings from Zoom **without signing in to Zoom**.
- **Speed:** ~90 seconds per 1 GB video.

## Why it's different
- Purpose-built for the **Zoom/Fathom → YouTube → LMS** path, not a generic connector.
- **Rule-based routing** so multi-course / multi-channel setups just work.
- **Full transfer log** — see exactly what published where, and re-push if needed.
- **The last upload always finishes** — runs never abort mid-transfer.
